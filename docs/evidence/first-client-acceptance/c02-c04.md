## Defects found and fixed (PR for #598)

1. **No tax rate could be created** (web). The form posted `accountId`, a number `rate` and
   `OUTPUT`/`INPUT`; the API takes `linkedAccountId`, a decimal string and `SALES`/`PURCHASES`/`BOTH`.
   The account pickers for tax rates, bank accounts and the general ledger also loaded only the first
   20 accounts, so 2200/2210 were not offered. Tests: `components/tax/tax-rate-form.spec.tsx`.
2. **A double click recorded a payment twice** (money). Receipts and supplier payments now carry an
   idempotency key: one per payment screen, stored under a unique (organization, key) index
   (migration `20261101050000_payment_idempotency_keys`). A retry returns the payment already
   recorded; a key reused with other details is refused (409, `PAYMENT_RETRY_MISMATCH`). Tests:
   `payments-received.service.spec.ts`, `payments-made.service.spec.ts`,
   `sales/payments/new/page.spec.tsx`.
3. **Seeded account names in English on Arabic screens** (API). Expense, bill-line, supplier-credit,
   refund, VAT and payment-detail account lookups, and the accounting trial balance and ledger, now
   select both languages so the existing interceptor answers in Arabic ("مصروف الإيجار", not "Rent
   Expense"). Tests: `expenses.service.spec.ts`, `accounting-reports.service.spec.ts`.

## Known issues (not fixed here)

- Voiding a receipt posts its reversal on the void day, and the screen offers no date. A duplicate
  found after the month ends stays in that month (here: bank +5,000 and AR −5,000 at 31 Oct until
  1 Nov). That is correct for a closed month, but an accountant cleaning up an open month may expect
  to choose the date.
- AR aging labels the credits netted at the cutoff "بعد خصم … من الإشعارات الدائنة غير المطبقة"
  even when the credit is an overpayment rather than a credit note (here the voided duplicate).
- Journal descriptions written by the server ("Payment received from …", "Vendor payment …",
  "Accounting for invoice …") are stored in English and show on the Arabic journals list.
