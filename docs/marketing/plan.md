# Mizano go-to-market plan (first paying pilots)

Written 1 Nov 2026 (session marketing). Free channels only, no paid ads, nothing is posted or sent by an agent: every
text here is a draft the owner publishes. Sources: `docs/research/competitors.md` and `docs/plans/first-client.md`
(31 Oct 2026), `docs/vision.md`, `docs/strategy/regional-compliance.md`. Where a number is not sourced it says
**unknown**; the targets below are things we aim to do, not forecasts of demand.

Companion files: `posts.ar.md`, `posts.en.md` (30 days of posts), `outreach.md` (first messages and follow-ups),
`demo-booking.md`. Visual prompts, the one-pager, demo script, objections and handbook come from session sales-kit in
`docs/sales/`; the posts name the prompt they need and link there instead of repeating it.

## 1. The promise (one sentence)

- AR: **ارفع فاتورة المورّد، فيجهّز ميزانو قيدًا مسودة بمصدره، ومحاسبك يراجع ويوافق قبل أن يُرحَّل أي شيء. بالعربية، ومجاني خلال الفترة التجريبية.**
- EN: **Upload a supplier bill and Mizano prepares a draft entry with its source; your accountant reviews and approves before anything is posted. Arabic first, and free during the pilot.**

Basis: `docs/vision.md` (a bill photo or PDF becomes a validated draft with its source evidence, then a posted entry
after authorized accountant batch approval) and `AGENTS.md` (no autonomous payment or statutory submission). Only
say it in the words above; a field-accuracy or speed figure is **unknown** until the held-out test in the roadmap
exists, so never quote one.

## 2. The ideal first-pilot customer

|                       |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Business type         | An **Egyptian small service business** (agency, clinic, contractor, consultancy, training centre) that books in EGP, receives supplier bills as paper, photos or PDFs, and has an accountant. This is the planning assumption in `first-client.md`, not observed demand. |
| Size                  | 3 to 25 people, one legal entity, one or two people touching the books. Unknown: how many such businesses still keep books in Excel or desktop software.                                                                                                                 |
| City                  | Cairo and Giza first (the accountant and the owner can meet), then Alexandria.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Who decides           | Two people: **the accountant** chooses the tool they work in every day, **the owner** agrees to pay or to adopt it. Start with the accountant: they have the pain and the authority to try something free. For an owner without an accountant, the owner decides alone.  |
| What they must have   | A taxpayer set-up the accountant already understands. The accountant tells us who issues the sales invoices legally (see section 6).                                                                                                                                     |
| What they must accept | A free pilot, one month of real bills in Mizano alongside the old system, a 15-minute demo, honest feedback.                                                                                                                                                             |
| Not a first pilot     | Businesses that need Mizano to **issue** Egyptian e-invoices (ETA) or Saudi ZATCA invoices (not built; `competitors.md`), retail with a cash register or POS, manufacturing, businesses that need payroll or inventory first.                                            |

Second audience, same product: **accountants and bookkeeping offices that keep the books of several small
businesses.** One yes there is the start of several pilots, and they feel the pain of re-typing bills most.

## 3. Three free channels most likely to reach them

1. **Accountants, one at a time, by personal message (WhatsApp and LinkedIn).** The pilot itself starts with 3 to 5
   invited accountants (`docs/vision.md`, invites 2 Nov 2026); everyone invited is a person the founder knows or was
   introduced to. Why: the accountant is the user and the door to their clients, and a 15-minute demo shows the one
   thing that matters (a bill becomes a draft that they approve). Templates: `outreach.md`.
2. **Facebook groups and WhatsApp communities of Egyptian accountants and small-business owners.** Why: this is where
   they ask each other which program to use and how to handle ETA questions. We answer questions first and post only
   where the rules allow it. Size and activity of each group are **unknown**; the plan counts conversations.
3. **The founder's LinkedIn profile with the 30 daily posts**, Arabic first, aimed at Egyptian business readers and
   finance professionals. Why: accountants and owners are on LinkedIn, and a post that explains one real problem (the
   bill in a drawer, the month-end rush) is a reason for someone to reply. Reach is **unknown**.

Not used: paid ads, bought lists, scraped numbers, auto-DM tools, group spam.

## 4. What the owner does each day (about 45 minutes)

1. Post the day's post from `posts.ar.md` (LinkedIn; Facebook where it fits).
2. Send 3 to 5 personal first messages (`outreach.md`), each with a real reason you picked that person.
3. Answer every reply the same day; move a yes to a booked demo (`demo-booking.md`).
4. Write one line in the learning log (section 5).

## 5. The 30-day plan: weekly targets are conversations

A **conversation** is a real two-way exchange with an accountant or owner who replied and said something about how they
do the books today. Posts, likes, views and followers are not goals.

| Week           | Focus                                                                                   | First messages sent | Conversations | Demos done | Pilots started                       |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 1 (days 1–7)   | The invited accountants and anyone they can introduce                                   | 15                  | 6             | 3          | 1                                    |
| 2 (days 8–14)  | Accountants with 5 or more small clients; join 3 groups and answer questions            | 20                  | 7             | 3          | 1                                    |
| 3 (days 15–21) | Owners introduced by those accountants; a pilot is one month of real bills side by side | 20                  | 6             | 3          | 1                                    |
| 4 (days 22–30) | Two follow-ups per person, then stop; read the log and decide what to change            | 15                  | 5             | 2          | 0 (target total 3 started by day 30) |

Effort targets for one person, not predictions. Learning log (private, no customer names in this repo): date, who
(accountant or owner), city, how they keep books today, what they do with a bill, objection in their words, next step.
At the end of week 2 and week 4 answer: which person replied most, which objection came up most, which post got a
reply; change the next week's messages. No conversations after 20 messages: change the message, not the volume.

**Stop rules**: no after two follow-ups, stop. "Remove me": confirm in one line, delete their details, never write again.

## 6. What we do not say

- No customers, testimonials, logos, accuracy figures, hours saved, or "trusted by". There are none to cite yet.
- No prices. Only: «مجاني خلال الفترة التجريبية» / "Free during the pilot".
- **Mizano does not issue Egyptian e-invoices (ETA) or Saudi ZATCA invoices today.** Say it before the demo ends, every
  time: the accountant confirms who issues sales invoices, and Mizano records them next to the books
  (`first-client.md`, "Choose the statutory issuer"). Never write "ETA ready" or "ZATCA compliant".
- No "zero-touch", "autonomous accounting" or "AI does your books". The accountant approves what posts; AI is off by
  default and works only through the organization's own account after consent (`AGENTS.md`, owner decision 31 Oct).
- Telegram intake and VAT return drafts are planned, not available; do not mention them as features.
- The login page still carries older English-and-Arabic claims ("32 AI models", "trusted by businesses"). These must
  not be repeated in posts and should be removed by the web owner (see `demo-booking.md` §6).

## 7. Where the path leads

Posts and messages end at one of two places that work without the owner's contact details: the sign-up page
(`/ar/register`, the free pilot) or the demo request page (`/ar/demo`, a form stored in our own database;
`demo-booking.md`).
