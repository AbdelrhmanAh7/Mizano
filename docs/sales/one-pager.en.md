# Mizano: Arabic and English bookkeeping for a small business and its accountant

_One-pager · 1 November 2026 · **Free during the pilot**_

## Who it is for
A small service business in Egypt (EGP books) with an owner and an accountant who keep the books themselves today, on paper,
in spreadsheets or in a system that does not speak Arabic well. Saudi Arabia and the UAE come later.

## The problem
Supplier bills arrive as photos and PDFs. Someone types every field again, checks the totals by hand, and finds the mistakes
at month end. Quotes, invoices, payments and the bank statement live in different places, so closing the month takes days and
nobody is sure the numbers match.

## What Mizano does
- **Sales:** customers, quotes, invoices (convert a quote to an invoice), credit notes and payments received, partial or full.
  Invoice and quote PDFs print in Arabic or English.
- **Purchases:** vendors, bills, expenses, payments made and vendor credits.
- **Scan a bill:** upload a photo or a PDF; Mizano reads it on its own server (no AI account needed), fills a bill for you to
  check, flags what it is not sure about, and warns about duplicates. Nothing is saved until you press **Create Bill**.
- **Approve and post:** draft bills are posted to the books only when a person approves them, one by one or several at once.
- **Bank:** import a bank statement (CSV, Excel, OFX/QFX) and match it to your records.
- **Reports:** profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, trial balance, general ledger, receivables and payables aging,
  sales and purchases by customer, item and vendor, and VAT returns.
- **Close the month:** lock the previous month so nobody changes it by mistake. Posted entries are never edited: a mistake is
  corrected by a linked reversal.
- **Your own AI, optional:** connect your Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or Gemini subscription with no key. It can read your books
  and prepare drafts; it never posts, sends, pays or deletes. Mizano works fully without it.

## What you get in the first week
1. Your organization set up: base currency EGP, fiscal year, chart of accounts (services, retail or construction template).
2. Your team added with the right roles (owner, accountant, sales).
3. Your first quote turned into an invoice, and a payment recorded against it.
4. Your first supplier bills scanned, checked and posted.
5. Your bank statement imported and matched.
6. A trial balance and a profit and loss you can read, in Arabic or English.

## How it differs (sourced, dated 31 October 2026, `docs/research/competitors.md`)
- **AI from your own subscription, without a key.** Qoyod's Claude integration is documented through Zapier, not a direct
  connection; Zoho Books documents an official Claude connector. Mizano connects Claude, ChatGPT, Codex and Gemini directly,
  read-only or read-and-draft, and the AI can never post or pay.
- **Bill reading without an AI account.** Mizano reads bills on its own server; an AI account is optional and off by default.
  Daftra and Wafeq advertise AI document scanners; we did not trial them.
- **Free during the pilot.** For comparison, Wafeq listed its Egypt Starter plan at EGP 966 a month (positioned as invoicing
  only) and Zoho Books' global page lists a free plan (not verified for Egypt); these are dated snapshots, not offers.
- **Not an e-invoicing issuer.** Daftra advertises both Egyptian ETA systems; Mizano does not issue e-invoices today (see limits).

## Honest limits today
- **No e-invoices to ETA (Egypt) or ZATCA (Saudi Arabia).** Keep your current authorized issuer; Mizano can record its ETA
  document number on the invoice for your books.
- **Bills come in by upload only**, one file at a time (images and PDF, up to 15 MB). Telegram intake is planned; WhatsApp and
  email intake are not.
- **"View source document"** on a journal entry opens the bill or invoice that created it, not the scanned picture.
- **Most report pages have no export button yet** (sales and purchases by customer, item and vendor export to CSV). Lists
  (invoices, bills, customers, vendors, expenses, journals) export to CSV or Excel, and invoices and quotes export to PDF.
- **No setup wizard:** setup is done in Settings, with us beside you during onboarding.
- Inventory, HR, CRM, projects and manufacturing screens exist but are **not part of the pilot**.

## Pilot terms
- **Free during the pilot.** No card needed.

- Mizano never pays, sends or files taxes on its own: a person approves what is posted.
