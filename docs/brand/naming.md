# Is "Mizano" the right name? Naming check, 1 November 2026

Owner brief (1 Nov 2026): names must be unique, attractive, reflect what the business does, be easy, and not collide with
competitors. Research only: **nothing was renamed and nothing was bought.** Renaming waits for the owner's yes.
Every check below was run on **2026-11-01** from the command line (DNS, whois, RDAP, GitHub API, iTunes Search API, page
reads, web search). "Not found" means searched and nothing came back; "not searched" means the source could not be read.
Companion report for the sister product: FlowLine repo, `docs/brand/naming.md`.

## 1. Verdict

**Replace "Mizano", once the owner confirms mizano.app is not theirs.** A live Arabic bookkeeping app for small businesses in
Saudi Arabia (registered 2026-05-24) answers to the same name (ميزانو) at mizano.app, which is exactly our category and one of our
two markets. Two more products named Mizano exist in other fields, and the stem «ميزان» is crowded in Arabic accounting.
Recommended replacement: **Sanduqi / صندوقي** (section 5). Trademark status of every name, ours and the alternatives, is
**unknown** (3.4).
The owner decides; this file changes no code.

## 5. Alternatives (screened 2026-11-01)

five below showed no live accounting product on .com, .ai, .app, .sa or .ae (page titles read) and no same-name accounting app
in the US, Egyptian or Saudi App Store (iTunes Search API); web search found nothing. Domain columns: **.ai/.sa** from
whois ("free" = no match); **.app** from RDAP via rdap.org (404 = not registered); **.ae** and **.eg**: no DNS records found, but those

Rejected after checking: ميزانية Mizaniya, ميزانيتي Mizaniti, حسابي Hisabi, قيد Qayd, رصيد Rasiid, حسيب Haseeb, تسوية Taswiya, دفترك Daftarak,
أرقام Arqam, خزين Khazeen (a storage company holds khazeen.sa; an app of that name exists), كاتب Katib, سجل Sijil.

**Recommendation: Sanduqi / صندوقي.** It is a word every shop owner and accountant already uses (the cash account), one spelling
in Arabic, three syllables, friendly to the small-business owner and still correct to the accountant, and nothing of the name
exists in accounting that we could find. Its limits are in the table (a till-like feel; the .com is a recent registration; .ae and .eg
unverified). If the owner wants a name that sounds more professional than warm, Khazin is the second choice. Before any
switch the owner confirms in a browser: WIPO and TMview, SAIP, UAE and Egypt registries, Google Play, handles.
