**What the owner should weigh:** جرد is the accountant's count and check (stock count, year-end reckoning), a good fit for month close and
reconciliation, but it is mainly a stock-count word (reconciliation is تسوية). Egyptians pronounce the ج as "g" ("Garda"), the Arabic
word is close to «جريدة» (newspaper), the .ai and .com domains are not available, and the Latin "Jarda" is also a Czech nickname.
It was chosen because it is the only screened name with no same-name product in our category and markets, not because it is perfect.

## 1. Verdict

**Replace "Mizano".** A live Arabic bookkeeping app for small businesses in Saudi Arabia (domain registered 2026-05-24) answers to the same
name (ميزانو) at mizano.app, which is exactly our category and one of our two markets. Two more products named Mizano exist in
other fields, and the stem «ميزان» is crowded in Arabic accounting. The owner confirmed the rename; the chosen name is in the
DECISION block above. The owner should still confirm that neither they nor a collaborator registered mizano.app.
Trademark status of every name, ours and the alternatives, is **unknown** (3.4).
