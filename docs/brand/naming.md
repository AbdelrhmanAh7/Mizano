Owner brief (1 Nov 2026): names must be unique, attractive, reflect what the business does, be easy, and not collide with
competitors. Research only: **nothing was renamed and nothing was bought**; the owner said yes to the rename on 1 Nov 2026, and the rename itself is a separate planned change.

Companion report for the sister product: FlowLine repo, `docs/brand/naming.md`.

## 1. Verdict

**Replace "Mizano".** A live Arabic bookkeeping app for small businesses in Saudi Arabia (registered 2026-05-24) answers to the same
name (ميزانو) at mizano.app, which is exactly our category and one of our two markets. Two more products named Mizano exist in
other fields, and the stem «ميزان» is crowded in Arabic accounting. The owner confirmed the rename; the chosen name is in the
DECISION block above. The owner should still confirm that neither they nor a collaborator registered mizano.app.
Trademark status of every name, ours and the alternatives, is **unknown** (3.4).

**Original recommendation (superseded by the DECISION block after the deeper checks): Sanduqi / صندوقي.** It is a word every shop owner and accountant already uses (the cash account), one spelling
in Arabic, three syllables, friendly to the small-business owner and still correct to the accountant, and nothing of the name
exists in accounting that we could find. Its limits are in the table (a till-like feel; the .com is a recent registration; .ae and .eg
unverified). If the owner wants a name that sounds more professional than warm, Khazin is the second choice. The deeper checks that followed rejected Sanduqi, Khazin and Iraad and chose Dakhli (DECISION block).
